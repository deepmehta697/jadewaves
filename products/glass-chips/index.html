Before you order

What should I include in a sample enquiry?
Choose a finish and listed chip grade, then share your application, binder system, quantity and destination. Your selection is included in the on-page enquiry; email is also available.
Can I use the photograph to approve grading?
Use the photograph for appearance only. Confirm the chip-size range and grading against an approved physical sample before ordering; a photograph is not a sieve report.
Is binder compatibility already confirmed?
No. Confirm suitability for your own terrazzo or surface system through sample assessment. A catalogue size or finish does not establish the performance of the completed system.
