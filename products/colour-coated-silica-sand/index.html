Before you order

How is this different from industrial silica sand?
This range is colour-coated decorative sand in a listed 0.5–1 mm grain size. Industrial silica sand chemistry and technical sheets do not establish the coating's performance or certify this decorative range.
What should be checked for my finish system?
State the intended use, binder system, target shade and grain-size requirement. Confirm coating performance and suitability against an approved sample. Aquarium decoration and children's play applications require specific suitability and safety verification.
What information is needed for a quotation?
Include your selected colour, 0.5–1 mm grade, quantity, destination, packing requirement and whether you need a sample. Confirm the final specification and packing before an order.
