Quartz Powder / Engineered Stone

Quartz Powder Supplier for Engineered Stone: What We Supply

A direct supply guide for engineered stone buyers reviewing available forms, sizes, quality focus, and export handling from India.

Jade Waves Enterprise / Buyer guidePublished 2026-04-11Quartz Sand technical reference ↗

What we supply for engineered stone buyers

If your requirement is quartz powder for engineered stone, the first step is to align the right form against your process, not only the product name.

  • Supply options include quartz sand, quartz grits, and quartz powder.
  • Grade and size range are aligned to engineered stone end use before quotation.
  • Commercial discussion is tied to form, packing basis, and shipment planning together.

Sizes available in the current supply range

Engineered stone buyers usually need both powder and controlled sized inputs depending on the production line and blend requirement.

  • 0.1-0.4 mm, 0.3-0.7 mm, 0.6-1.2 mm, 1.2-2.5 mm, 2.5-4.0 mm, and 4.0-6.0 mm
  • 200 mesh, 325 mesh, and 400 mesh
  • Custom size discussion available against application requirement

Quality focus before quotation

For engineered stone programs, technical alignment needs to happen before pricing so the offer matches production needs.

  • SiO2 above 99.0 percent with low potassium profile
  • Form and grain or mesh size alignment before sample movement
  • Color, whiteness, and consistency discussion before commercial finalization

Packing and export handling

Shipment planning matters as much as technical fit because engineered stone buyers often need the same basis to hold through trial and repeat orders.

  • Current packing basis: jumbo bags
  • Current shipment mode: FCL
  • Sample alignment, document alignment, and shipment planning are handled from India based on destination and order size

RFQ checklist

Questions for your first inquiry.

  • End use, slab line, and process requirement
  • Required form and size range: powder, grits, or sand
  • SiO2 target, whiteness expectation, and key impurity limits
  • Trial quantity and expected monthly volume
  • Destination port and preferred packing basis
