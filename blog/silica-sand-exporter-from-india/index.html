Silica Sand / Export Supply

Silica Sand Exporter from India: What We Supply

A direct supply guide for import buyers reviewing silica sand forms, application fit, quality focus points, and export handling from India.

Jade Waves Enterprise / Buyer guidePublished 2026-04-11Updated 2026-10-10Silica Sand technical reference ↗

What we offer

If your team is sourcing silica sand from India, the first step is to align the right supply form against application and shipment basis.

  • Current silica line includes silica sand and silica flour
  • Supply discussion is aligned to end use before quotation
  • Packing and shipment planning are handled together with the grade discussion

Where the silica line is used

Silica requirements change by process, so application fit needs to be clear before commercial discussion.

  • Glass manufacturing
  • Water filtration media
  • Foundry moulding systems
  • Paints, construction, and specialty fillers

Quality focus before quotation

Technical alignment matters most when import teams want trials and repeat shipments to stay on the same basis.

  • Grain size, SiO2 content, and Fe2O3 requirement are aligned first
  • For glass use, Fe2O3 requirements can be discussed against the required grade
  • Packing and order volume are handled together with quality alignment

Supply and export setup

The practical side of the order matters just as much as the material itself when shipment planning starts.

  • Packing: 50 Kg bags and jumbo bags
  • Shipment mode: FCL
  • Export handling includes sample alignment, document alignment, and shipment planning from India based on destination and order size

RFQ checklist

Questions for your first inquiry.

  • End use: glass, foundry, filtration, construction, or another line
  • Required grain size or mesh range
  • SiO2 target and Fe2O3 limit for the intended process
  • Trial quantity and expected monthly requirement
  • Destination port, packing basis, and delivery timing
